Sinigang na Baboy

A Classic Filipino Sour Pork Soup

Ingredients

  • ½ kilo pork belly or pork ribs
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 radish, sliced
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 1 cup string beans
  • 2 cups kangkong leaves
  • 1 packet sinigang mix
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce (patis)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Procedure

  1. Wash the pork and place it in a pot with 6 cups of water.
  2. Bring to a boil, then simmer until the pork becomes tender.
  3. Add the onion and tomatoes. Cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add the fish sauce and sinigang mix. Stir well.
  5. Add the radish and cook for about 5 minutes.
  6. Add the eggplant and string beans.
  7. Continue cooking until the vegetables are tender.
  8. Add the kangkong leaves and cook for 1–2 minutes.
  9. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  10. Serve hot with steamed rice.
Cooking Tip: Adjust the amount of sinigang mix to get your preferred level of sourness.
